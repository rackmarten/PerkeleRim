MSC voice picker

My Summer Car's sound clips from your own install, grouped the way MSC's MasterAudio groups them (Hangover, Swearing, Drunk, Teimo…), then clips other scene objects play, then clips nothing plays directly. Press ▶ to listen, copy to copy a line's reference (like Hangover #3 (hangover03)) for telling a session which line you mean, and pick to collect several and copy them all at once from the bar at the bottom.

Keys: / search, ↓↑ or jk move, Space or Enter play/stop, c copy, p pick, Esc stop and leave the search.
