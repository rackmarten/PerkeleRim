# Changelog

All notable changes to this project are documented here.

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and this project adheres to [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Skyrim's food and drink feed Kumpo: eating lowers My Summer Car's hunger, drinks lower thirst, and ale, mead, wine, brandy and Skooma raise his drunk level by strength. A bottle of alcohol leaves an empty bottle in the inventory, and once the inventory is closed Kumpo drinks it in My Summer Car (once, however many he had). MSC Beer and MSC's food can be eaten and drunk from Skyrim's inventory too.
- Every drink has its own bottle: once the inventory is closed Kumpo drinks ale and mead from My Summer Car's beer bottle, wine, brandy and Sujamma from its booze bottle, Skooma from its spirit bottle (without My Summer Car's chance of going blind), milk from the carton and juice from the juice. Each bottle of alcohol leaves its own empty: Empty Bottle for ale and mead, Empty Wine Bottle, Empty Liquor Bottle and Empty Skooma Bottle.
- Drunk by bottle: a mead gets Kumpo a little drunker than an ale, a whole bottle of wine about three beers, brandy and Sujamma like My Summer Car's booze bottle.
- Soft drinks: My Summer Car's juice goes in the pocket (Y) and is drunk from Skyrim's inventory, lowering thirst with no drunk; other mods' soft drinks do the same. Crouching in water and pressing E takes a sip of water.
- Gold is Kumpo's money: My Summer Car's money follows Skyrim's gold both ways (one mark per gold), and shows as Money under the needs bars. The Skyrim save is the truth, so loading a save brings its money back.
- Skyrim's favorites hotkeys are Kumpo's hotbar: three new powers (Empty Hands, Tools, Throw Empty Bottle), MSC Beer, MSC's food and any Skyrim food or drink can be bound to 1 to 8 in Skyrim's Favorites menu, which R opens on foot. The first time a save meets PerkeleRim its favorites get 1 Empty Hands, 2 Tools, 3 Throw Empty Bottle, 4 MSC Beer and 5 to 8 MSC's food; unbound numbers do nothing. Beer and food work with the tools out (Kumpo puts them away for a drink and takes them back out), and the tools icon shows top right while they're out. 3 is a throwing stance: after a throw the next empty bottle comes out by itself, and switching to another number puts the bottle in hand away first.
- Play mode: no more F10. Loading a save (the PerkeleRim-Kumpo starter, your own, a quickload, the reload after a death) hands Kumpo to My Summer Car by itself a couple of seconds after it has loaded, and again after quitting to the main menu and loading another one. If My Summer Car isn't running, Skyrim says so and Kumpo goes over once it is (also when MSC is still loading, or drops out mid-game). F10 stays as a debug toggle (protocol v41).
- Kumpo's Helgen intro, from the most liked comment on the PerkeleRim video: Kumpo drank one beer too many in My Summer Car and wakes up in Skyrim's Helgen cart. A new game opens on black with him snoring, then groaning MSC's own hangover line ("Damn, that was definitely the last time I drink anything. Oh satan, what a feeling."), and after Ralof's "Hey, you. You're finally awake." he says Perkele. Captions show each line. Helgen plays with Skyrim's own controls; Kumpo goes over to My Summer Car once he's out of the keep's cave, where he stands (the van parks next to him). Saves that never had the handover still wake him in the shack bed by the garage, with the same hangover line, so both start the same way: a night out, then Skyrim.
- Kumpo's thu'um: Unrelenting Force yells MSC's own Perkele a syllable per word of power, "PER" for one word, "PER-KE" for two and "PER-KE-LE!" for all three. The clip is cut at runtime from your own My Summer Car, nothing from the game is shipped.
- Other common shouts have a Finnish word of their own: Whirlwind Sprint "Vittu!", Fire Breath "Mitä helvettiä!?", Frost Breath "Voi vittu!", Become Ethereal "Jumalauta.", Dragonrend "Saatana!", Slow Time "Saatana...", Marked for Death "Kyrpä!", Clear Skies "Voi saatana!", Storm Call "Jumalauta jo!". Any other shout is still a random swear.
- The drunker Kumpo is, the shorter Skyrim's shout cooldown, down to a third of it at full drunk: a full Fus Ro Dah is back in 15 s instead of 45.
- Showing the middle finger (V) to a guard or a Jarl within about 5 m in front of you adds a 5 gold bounty in their hold, once per person per minute.
- Dwemer oil stations beside Skyrim's main roads, by the garage outside Whiterun, south-east of Dragon Bridge, by the bridge below Windhelm and between Ivarstead and Riften, each on the map. Fill up the My Summer Car way: stop by the dwemer pump, open the fuel hatch, unscrew the cap, take the nozzle and pour. Every litre costs gold as it pours, at My Summer Car's own diesel price, and the pour stops when the gold runs out. The HUD shows the litres, the gold and the tank as it fills, and the hose reaches 6 m, so the tractor can fill up too.
- Fuel runs out: the van and the Kekmet burn their diesel as in My Summer Car (the moped's tank stays full). The HUD warns under a quarter of a tank. A dry vehicle won't start, and calling it only works at a station, where it's towed onto the pump for 50 gold (free if it's already within 100 m) with its parking brake pulled.
- Vehicles are solid in Skyrim: the van, the moped and the Kekmet stop arrows, spells and flames (hiding behind the parked van works, and arrows hit it with a metal clang) and people walk around them instead of through them. Ragdolls and dropped items bounce off too. Kumpo himself still walks through, and NPCs can't see him behind the van. At driving speed (from about 7 km/h) the hitbox steps aside so running people over works as before; Kumpo stays safe in the van and the Kekmet while seated, and in the van now also once he has climbed in before pressing Enter.
- Setup in the launcher: the first time, Start PerkeleRim.bat finds My Summer Car and Skyrim (Steam libraries, Steam's own install records, or folders you pick), lists what's missing of MSCLoader, SKSE and Address Library, opens their download pages and waits while you install them, then installs PerkeleRim into both games. Skyrim's SKSE build is checked against the Skyrim version too.
- Mod Organizer 2: when MO2 manages your Skyrim, the launcher asks once whether to start Skyrim through it; then Address Library may be an MO2 mod, `PerkeleRim.esp` is ticked in MO2's selected profile, and an older PerkeleRim installed as an MO2 mod gets a warning.

### Changed

- Saves from 0.1.x keep their MSC Beer, Sausages, Pizza, Potato Chips and Macaron Box: they become food items under the same IDs (tested in game on a 0.1.1 save, which loaded them and ate and drank them from the inventory).

### Removed

- Endless fuel for the van and the Kekmet.
- The endless pocket (beer, bottles and food topped back up) and mead and ale turning into MSC Beer when picked up: mead stays mead and is drunk in Skyrim.

### Fixed

- Kumpo's hangover goes with the Skyrim save: loading a save brings back that save's hangover, or none, instead of keeping the camera shaking from the hangover he had before the load.
- Sleeping in a Skyrim bed while Kumpo is in My Summer Car counts as sleep again, not waiting: 8 hours in a bed take his fatigue to 0 (it was read as waiting, which takes only part of it).
- The needs bars show up to 200, where a need kills Kumpo, with a mark at 100 where they turn red, so a beer or a sleep that takes 30 off a need above 100 shows.
- My Summer Car started from Steam after a PerkeleRim session keeps your own resolution and graphics quality: the launcher puts them back once MSC closes (PerkeleRim runs MSC in a small window at its lowest quality, and Unity kept that). If an earlier PerkeleRim already left MSC at 640x480, set your resolution and quality in MSC once more and they stay.
- MSCLoader's console (~) stays quiet during play: it shows only the link coming up or going down and anything that failed. Every PerkeleRim line still goes to `output_log.txt`.
- The launcher no longer stops at a Steam library on a drive that isn't there any more.

## [0.1.1] - 2026-10-22

### Added

- Skyrim Special Edition 1.5.97 support, tested in game. The SKSE plugin was already one DLL for SE and AE; the launcher now checks for the SE Address Library file (`version-1-5-97-0.bin`) on 1.5.x, and doesn't add the starter Skyrim save there (it was made on AE and 1.5.97 calls it corrupt).

### Fixed

- `PerkeleRim.esp` is written with header version 1.70 instead of 1.71, which Skyrim SE 1.5.97 skipped without a word.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-19

First public release.

### Added

- Van and tractor fixes from a steep-hill drive: the van grips harder uphill (forward grip 1.8x MSC's); a vehicle that sinks into the island goes back where it last stood on its wheels (within 60 m), or onto a surface at most 4 m above it, never onto a cliff far above; a vehicle that comes back from a save inside a wall or rock is stood upright on the nearest clear, flat spot before it is let go; Kumpo can always get out of a van or tractor tipped past 60 degrees, and at a walking pace otherwise, like the moped. The van's thin nose collider no longer digs into downhills (its bottom is lifted), and every vehicle is pushed out of geometry at 4 m/s at most, not thousands of km/h.
- A starter Skyrim save in the release zip: Kumpo, a male Nord just out of Helgen. The launcher adds it as PerkeleRim-Kumpo next to the player's own saves, never over one (`scripts/starter-save.ps1 -Skyrim` makes it).
- Wading into water up to Kumpo's waist washes him: his dirtiness goes to 0, once per wade in (ankle- or knee-deep does nothing; getting out re-arms it). Swimming, drowning and vehicles in water are still ignored.
- A throw from sneak at someone who has not noticed Kumpo (Skyrim's detection level 0 or below) deals double damage on top of the speed rule, with Skyrim's "Sneak attack for 2.0x damage" message. Punches stay as they are.
- Repository scaffold: README, MIT license, contributing guide, code of conduct, security policy, issue and PR templates.
- Design document ([docs/DESIGN.md](docs/DESIGN.md)) and protocol proposal ([docs/PROTOCOL.md](docs/PROTOCOL.md)).
- Windows development setup guide ([docs/DEV_SETUP.md](docs/DEV_SETUP.md)) and `scripts/build.ps1`, `deploy-local.ps1`, `logs.ps1`.
- Hello-world SKSE plugin (CMake, vcpkg, CommonLibSSE-NG) and MSCLoader mod (`net35`, `dotnet build`) that log when they load.
- GitHub Actions build on `windows-latest`; the C# mod compiles against MSCLoader API stubs there.
- Hidden-MSC spike ([docs/SPIKE_HIDDEN_MSC.md](docs/SPIKE_HIDDEN_MSC.md)): MSC input redirect and FSM dump tools, Skyrim per-frame player placement test, PlayMaker FSM map ([docs/FSM_MAP.md](docs/FSM_MAP.md)).
- Dev loop scripts: `scripts/launch-msc.ps1` (no-click MSC start), `msc-save.ps1` (save snapshots), `spike.ps1` (commands and input scripts for the running game).
- `scripts/restart-games.ps1`: closes both games, optionally builds, deploys, starts both and waits for `link up`.

- Protocol v1 link ([docs/PROTOCOL.md](docs/PROTOCOL.md)): shared memory section with version handshake and heartbeat, latest-value slots for input and player state, one ring per direction, Skyrim <-> Unity coordinate conversion. C++ in `protocol/` (used by the SKSE plugin), C# mirror in `msc/src/Link`. Both games log `link up` when running together.
- Unit tests for both ends (`scripts/test.ps1`, also run in CI): C++ doctest, C# NUnit, layout checked against the C++ header's asserts, shared conversion vectors.
- Milestone 1a, live player loop: F10 in Skyrim hands the player to MSC. Skyrim forwards keyboard and mouse as `InputState` (scan codes mapped to Unity key codes, Escape/Tab/~ stay with Skyrim) and turns off its own movement, look, sneak, jump, attack and POV controls; MSC builds a flat placeholder collision island, plays the player on it from that input and reports `PlayerState`, and Skyrim places its player there every frame. MSC's Escape menu closes itself while handed over, MSC crouch/prone becomes Skyrim sneak, and MSC movement speeds are scaled by 2.75 while handed over (Harmony prefix on the character motor, with ground acceleration raised to match). Unit tests for the key mapping, input decoding and island math.
- Milestone 1b, real Skyrim ground: while MSC owns the player, the SKSE plugin samples Skyrim's collision around it (top-down Havok raycasts on a 64-unit grid, 5 x 5 chunks of 2048 units, terrain, statics and tree trunks kept, clutter, actors and water passed through) and streams `CollisionChunk` heightfields; MSC builds a MeshCollider per chunk on the island, replacing the flat placeholder, and drops chunks the player walked away from. Chunks with holes are sampled again. A new anchor (handover, load door, fast travel) clears the island and MSC holds the player until the ground under it has arrived. Unit tests for chunk building and parsing.

- Milestone 2a, driving on Skyrim ground: on every handover MSC parks the Hayosiko van to the player's right and the Jonnez moped to the left, on the collision island, with keys and full tanks. The van is reported to Skyrim as `VehicleState` and drawn there with a vanilla cart mesh as a stand-in (no collision). A vehicle the player sits in comes along to a new anchor; one with no island ground under it is held until the ground arrives. Island chunks copy MSC's asphalt (layer, surface tag and physics material), so the wheels grip. While the player moves fast, Skyrim also samples collision up to 2 s ahead of it. MSC logs its ground surfaces once per game load.
- Milestone 2b, the real van in Skyrim: `tools/convert-vehicle.ps1` converts the Hayosiko from your own My Summer Car install into a Skyrim SE NIF with DDS textures (AssetStudioMod libraries, texconv, Blender + PyNifly; `tools/setup.ps1` fetches the tools, output is never committed, see [tools/README.md](tools/README.md)). Skyrim draws it instead of the cart (the cart stays as a fallback), with the front wheels steered and all four spun from `VehicleState`, which MSC now fills from its wheel models. While driving, Skyrim lowers the player so its camera sits where MSC's seated camera is.
- Milestone 2c, the garage near Whiterun: `scripts/build-esp.ps1 [-Deploy]` generates `PerkeleRim.esp` from your own `Skyrim.esm` with `tools/esp-gen` (Mutagen; this tool alone is GPL-3.0) and enables it. It places an open shed with plank walls (vanilla pieces) on the tundra south-west of the Whiterun stables, a spawn marker in front and a van marker inside. The first F10 of every save moves the player to the garage and hands over there with the van parked inside (protocol v4: `WorldspaceChange` can name where the van parks); the SKSE cosave remembers it, later handovers start where the player stands. The collision sampler looks through the garage, so its roof doesn't become a solid block on MSC's island.
- Milestone 2d, the van's moving parts: protocol v5 adds a `VehicleParts` slot carrying, for a fixed list of 40 van parts, each part's pose relative to the body and whether MSC shows it. Skyrim now shows the doors opening, the steering wheel, key, pedals, levers, wipers, fuel hatch, radio tuner and seatbelt moving, the gauge needles following speed, fuel and temperature, and the head, brake, reverse and blinker lights and dashboard lamps switching on and off (they glow). The converted NIF keeps each part as its own node; reconvert the van with `tools/convert-vehicle.ps1 -Deploy`.
- MSC's camera and look-at text in Skyrim: protocol v6 and v7 add MSC's camera pose to `PlayerState` (eye relative to the feet, roll, field of view) and a `LookAtText` message (text plus MSC's hand icon). Skyrim's first-person camera now sits exactly where MSC's does, so leaning (Q/E), reaching for the door while seated, crouching and the Ctrl zoom all show, and the camera no longer pivots around Skyrim's head. This replaces lowering the player into the seat. While MSC owns the player, Skyrim draws a small dot at the screen center and MSC's interaction prompt below it (its hand icon on doors and hatches, text such as "IGNITION" or item names), and Q no longer opens Skyrim's favorites.
- Milestone 3a, MSC's HUD and the scroll wheel: protocol v8. The mouse wheel reaches MSC as an accumulated `mouseWheel` in `InputState` (MSC's `Mouse ScrollWheel` axis), so the radio tuner and other knobs work. `PlayerState` carries MSC's needs (thirst, hunger, stress, urine, fatigue, dirtiness, drunk level), shown as MSC-style bars bottom left in Skyrim's HUD with urine always highlighted, and a new `Subtitle` message brings MSC's subtitle line (the swearing) to the bottom of the screen. The van's radio needle replaces the radio tuner pivot in the parts list, so the dial moves in Skyrim; reconvert the van with `tools/convert-vehicle.ps1 -Deploy`.
- Milestone 3b, beer and mead: protocol v9. Mead and ale picked up in Skyrim (Nord Mead, Ale, Honningbrew, Black-Briar and its Reserve; quest meads stay) leave Skyrim's inventory and reach MSC as a new `GiveBeer` message: the bottles go into the nearest of the player's beer cases with drunk bottles, or a new case appears next to the player with just that many, once MSC has the player. MSC's drunk level drives a looping blur with double vision in Skyrim, full at level 4 like the drunk bar. PerkeleRim.esp (rebuild with `scripts/build-esp.ps1 -Deploy`) adds the drink list and the blur. Skyrim resends a sneak start or stop until its animation graph follows MSC's stance (it used to stay crouched after a quick crouch and stand). Spike command `beer <count>` hands out beer in MSC.
- Milestone 3d spike, MSC models in Skyrim: protocol v10. MSC reads an object's meshes and textures from the running game and sends them as a new `ModelChunk` message; Skyrim builds them in memory (no offline conversion, nothing written to disk) and draws them with Skyrim's lighting. The first beer case goes once MSC gets the player and stands in front of it in Skyrim. Spike commands `model <name>` and `textures <name>`. Unit tests for the model blob on both ends.
- Milestone 3e, MSC items in Skyrim: protocol v11 adds an `ItemState` slot. MSC reports the physics items within 15 m of the player (its `(itemx)` items, anything under `ITEMS`, and small loose rigidbodies such as a thrown bottle) every frame, and sends each item's model once, shared by identical items. A beer case's bottles are items of their own, so a drunk bottle disappears and an emptied case shows empty. Skyrim draws each item where MSC has it, so carrying, dropping and throwing look right, and clears them on F10 or an anchor change. Items near the player come along to the next anchor (the next handover, a load door) so they stay where they were in Skyrim, held in place until the ground under them has arrived. Models are sent again after the link reconnects. MSC's models now get a flat normal map with no specular (they shone white in the sun, like the van once did) and no longer cast shadows (thin shapes shadowed themselves in streaks). The spike's "first beer case in front of the player" and command `model <name>` are gone; new spike command `items` lists what Skyrim gets.
- Milestone 3c/3f, damage, death and MSC's materials: protocol v12. Skyrim's health is the only health: while handed over MSC's player never dies (its `DEATH` event is caught, `Systems/Death` is switched off again, and a driver thrown out through the windshield is put back in the seat). A crash MSC would die of costs half of Skyrim's max health (`PlayerDamage`), and falls cost Skyrim's vanilla fall damage, measured from MSC's landing. When the player dies, Skyrim shows MSC's blood over the view (its own texture, sent at runtime), MSC plays its death sound (`PlayerDeath`), and Skyrim's normal death and reload follow; MSC's player stays alive. Skyrim's health shows as a red row under MSC's needs bars, since Skyrim hides its own meter while MSC has the player. Models carry MSC's materials: cutout (the beer case's grid), blended, unlit, highlight and glow, with MSC's color tint and metal baked into the texture. Spike commands `death` (MSC's `DEATH` event), `grunt` and `shot` (MSC's view to a PNG); `textures <name>` now also saves the textures it reads. An empty `PerkeleRim_testbeer.txt` in MSC's folder hands out a case on every F10.
- MSC's hands in Skyrim: protocol v13. MSC renders its own first-person layer (what its `HandCamera` draws: hands, the bottle being drunk, tools in hand, gestures) into a texture shared with Skyrim on the GPU, and Skyrim shows it full screen under its HUD, so drinking, punching, the hitchhike thumb and the middle finger look exactly like MSC. About 0.3 ms per frame in MSC at 1080p. MSC must run with `-force-gfx-direct` (the launch script passes it). Spike commands `hands`, `handlayer on|off|tint|save`, `event <NAME>` (a global PlayMaker event, e.g. `DRINKBEER`) and `global <name> <value>`; `items` also logs each item's layer. `scripts/skyrim-key.ps1` presses a key in Skyrim (F10 to hand over) and takes screenshots.
- Crashes MSC survives cost Skyrim health too: MSC measures each impact on the vehicle the player drives (its change in velocity, gravity taken out) and sends `PlayerDamage` scaled by it. Bumps up to 4 m/s cost nothing, harder hits up to 40% of max health at 12 m/s, one hit per second. When MSC calls the same hit fatal, only its death counts. Every impact from 2 m/s up is logged (`crash:`) for tuning.
- MSC's hands follow Skyrim's light: MSC renders the hand layer under fixed neutral lighting and Skyrim tints it with its own light at the player, so the hands are day-lit by day, warm at sunset and dim at night or in dark interiors, whatever MSC's clock says. The piss stream now shows in Skyrim (its particles write alpha in the hand layer) and is no longer cut off at 5 m. Spike command `handlayer light <ambient> <sun>`.
- Skyrim saves keep MSC's state: protocol v14 (`MscSave`, `MscLoad`). Every Skyrim save writes a snapshot of MSC's state next to it in a few ms (needs, money, time of day, and while MSC has the player the van, the moped and the items nearby with beer case bottle counts), and loading that save, including the reload after a death, puts it back in place without reloading MSC. A save taken while MSC had the player hands it over again after loading, with the vehicles where they were. MSC's own save still happens when it quits. Snapshots of deleted saves are removed, also when a save manager keeps saves in subfolders. F5 and F9 no longer reach MSC while it has the player (F9 switched MSC to its cinematic camera, which broke clicking). `scripts/skyrim-key.ps1` presses F5/F9; spike commands `snapshot save|load <name>` and `pick` (what MSC's mouse ray hits).
- No-click test loop: `scripts/launch-msc.ps1` clicks "Play!" on Unity's configuration dialog itself (it never found the dialog before) and starts MSC at 640x480 on the lowest quality. With `PerkeleRim_autoload.txt` in Skyrim's folder, the SKSE plugin loads the newest save from the main menu and answers the "Creations are currently loaded" box.
- Real static collision on MSC's island: buildings, walls, doors, rocks, bridges, tree trunks and interior rooms now reach MSC as their own Havok collision triangles (read straight from the loaded references' shapes, under 1 ms per chunk), next to a land-only heightfield. Doorways and archways are open and bridges can be driven under, so exterior doors (Whiterun's houses) can be reached, and interiors get their floors and walls instead of their ceiling. Land rays now also keep the ground layer where it has no reference, which some cells use for their landscape. The garage no longer needs its roof hidden from the sampler. MSC finds the ground under a bridge or rock (it saw only the first surface per chunk), and a player who slips below the island (stepping out of the van into a rock) is put back on top. Spike command `sink <m>` pushes the player down to test that. A hard hit no longer breaks the joint each driver seat hangs by while handed over (held every frame, since MSC sets the van's to break at 300 every frame): the broken joint threw the driver out as a loose body that fell through the island (Skyrim then started new anchors over and over) or shook the camera without end. Such a hit now costs what the crash sensor measures (up to 40%) instead of half the health. A seated player more than 6 m from its vehicle is logged and caught like any other fall.
- Milestone 4a, talking and doors: E activates in Skyrim (doors, people, quest items) unless MSC's crosshair is on something of its own. In dialogue and other menus with a cursor MSC gets no keys and its camera stands still. The island now also holds railings, plank floors and drawbridges (boxes Skyrim places with a transform of their own, read with Havok 2010's layout and checked to be a rotation), the transparent layer (railings, grates, invisible walls), and clutter that doesn't move (doors without a load door, chests, tables). A door that opens or closes has its chunks read again once it has swung, so an opened cell door lets you through. Vehicles stay in the worldspace or interior they were left in, so going through a load door no longer brings the van along into Dragonsreach; Skyrim snapshots keep where they are.
- Milestone 5a, people and punching: Skyrim's actors near the player stand on MSC's island as capsules the player bumps into (protocol v15, `ActorList`). MSC's punch (its Hit key, or left click with free hands) lands on them as `PunchEvent`, and Skyrim hits the actor as the player for a quarter of its max health with a stagger (PerkeleRim.esp's punch spells), so the target fights back and hitting someone peaceful is assault.
- Healing: what MSC's player consumes heals Skyrim's health (protocol v16, `Consumed`): beer and shots 10% of max health, kilju 15%, booze 20%, spirit 25%, a cigarette 5% when lit, food 25%, never past the max. MSC reads the finished drink, the lit cigarette and the eaten food from PlayMaker's state changes. Drunk punches hit harder: a quarter more per MSC drunk level, up to twice as hard at drunk 4.
- The pocket: Skyrim's inventory carries MSC's beer, empty bottles, food and cigarettes (protocol v17, `PocketChange`, `PocketCounts`; seven new PerkeleRim.esp misc items, rebuild it with `scripts/build-esp.ps1 -Deploy`). While handed over, Y pockets what MSC's hand carries, or with empty hands what the crosshair is on (one bottle of a beer case, an empty bottle, sausages, pizza, potato chips, a macaron box). Keys 3 to 8 use the pocket: 3 drinks a beer with MSC's own animation, 5 to 8 eat food at once, 4 takes an empty bottle out into the hand (again puts it back); 1 and 2 stay MSC's. MSC's cigarettes and Skyrim's Cigarette items stay equal both ways (MSC's snapshot no longer restores them). Mead and ale picked up in Skyrim now become MSC Beer in the inventory instead of a beer case in MSC (`GiveBeer` retired). Skyrim's HUD lists the pocket next to the needs bars. The test flag `PerkeleRim_testbeer.txt` now tops the pocket up on every handover (6 beer, 2 sausages, 1 pizza, 20 cigarettes) instead of filling beer cases. Spike commands `pocket`, `hold` and `inspect` (tag, layer, physics and FSMs of objects by name, inactive ones too).
- Thrown items and piss as attacks (protocol v18, `ImpactEvent`; a new PerkeleRim.esp poison spell, rebuild it with `scripts/build-esp.ps1 -Deploy`). An item thrown out of the hand that hits a Skyrim actor lands as a punch scaled by its speed (nothing at 2 m/s, a full sober punch from 15 m/s), with a stagger. Bottles fly twice as fast as MSC throws them, so they work as a ranged attack, dragons included. Piss is poison: while the stream is on someone they take 2% of their max health per second (dragons a quarter of that), running on 5 s after it leaves, and like any attack it counts as assault. The test stock (`PerkeleRim_testbeer.txt`) now also tops the pocket up to 10 empty bottles to throw (key 4 takes one out). A beer drunk from the pocket (key 3) puts its empty bottle back in the pocket instead of throwing it on the ground; a beer from a held case keeps MSC's toss.
- Running people over (protocol v19, `kImpactVehicle`). The van and moped drive through Skyrim's actors instead of stopping dead on them, and the driver takes no crash damage for it. From about 7 km/h the hit knocks the person over along the way the vehicle was going, harder the faster it went, and costs them health by speed: about a third of their max health at 20 km/h, dead from 50 km/h (dragons take a quarter). People and small animals are flung ahead of the van; anything of 250 kg or more (bears, cows, horses) stops the van like a wall, which hurts the driver, and only falls over; giants, mammoths and dragons don't even fall. Like any attack it counts as assault.
- Vehicles that hold Skyrim's roads: while on the island the van and moped are 20% heavier with a lower center of mass, the van's suspension is a bit softer with 20% more travel (MSC's own UnityCar settings, nothing saved): much harder to roll. The van's body collider (`CarCollider`) has its bottom 0.25 m higher, since its wheels sit far behind the bumpers: the overhang clips into lips and ramps and the wheels climb them, instead of the bumper stopping the van dead. A crash in the log now says which part of the vehicle hit what. MSC's push key (J) next to a rolled vehicle that has come to rest puts it back on its wheels.
- Ramps over short steps on the island: MSC samples each collision chunk's top surface on a 25 cm grid, and wherever a level deck (about a meter wide or more) sits 3 cm to 0.8 m above the ground beside it (a bridge deck's edge, the land climbing to it, a curb, a hole in the road), lays a 1 in 5 ramp down from it as extra collision triangles, so the van's wheels roll over instead of the body slamming into the step. Taller drops stay walls. The ramp raises the ground beside the step by the step's height (measured down to its foot, where the ground stops falling steeper than about 27 degrees), less 1 in 5 going out, so at the top of a hill it follows the hill down and fades out instead of ending in a shelf.

### Changed

- Alt pisses on a quick tap instead of on press: Skyrim sends it to MSC only when Alt was held no longer than 0.35 s, with no other key pressed meanwhile and Skyrim still in front, so Alt+Tab no longer starts a stream. Once started it runs until Kumpo is empty.
- Sleeping in a Skyrim bed takes fatigue off twice as fast as MSC's bed, so 8 hours zero it; waiting without a bed is unchanged.
- Punches deal Skyrim's own unarmed damage (race, Fortify Unarmed, perks such as Fists of Steel, difficulty) times 2 instead of a quarter of the target's max health, and up to twice that drunk; thrown items scale off the sober punch. The punch reaches 2 m and small creatures (mudcrabs, skeevers) can be hit standing. Thrown items hit twice as hard as that sober punch at full speed, since a throw is hard to aim.
- Drunk scales to MSC's own slow drunk level (five beers about 0.4, two dozen about 2.5): Skyrim's drunk blur stays off below 1.5 and stays light. Drinks heal less (beer 3%, spirit 8%, food 15%).
- Fuel is endless: every vehicle's tank is kept full, since Skyrim has no fuel stations yet and MSC's fuel gauge isn't visible there.
- Vehicles plow through people and animals alike, bears, elk and cows too: only huge actors (2000 kg or more by their bounds: mammoths, every dragon) stop them. Heavy animals are now thrown like people. While the player drives, the light capsules are off entirely, so a person can never stop the van.

- F10 switches Skyrim's camera to first person when it hands the player over (in third person Finn's hands and the whole Skyrim character showed) and back to third person when it takes the player back, if it was there before.
- Protocol v3: `VehicleSlot` (latest `VehicleState`, MSC -> Skyrim) after the player slot, which moves both rings. Quaternion conversion between Unity and Skyrim space, with shared test vectors. Both halves must be updated together.

- Protocol v2: `PlayerState.generation` (the anchor a pose belongs to; Skyrim ignores poses for older anchors) and `kCollisionChunkUnits`. Both halves must be updated together.

- `scripts/launch-msc.ps1` starts MSC with single-threaded rendering (`-force-gfx-direct`) and presses Space on the news screen as a real key press (MSC ignores posted key messages in that mode).

- `scripts/launch-msc.ps1` keeps watching for Unity's configuration dialog until the game window appears (it can take over 10 s on a cold start).

### Fixed

- Skyrim's sneak eye no longer stays up after Kumpo stands: the HUD hides it every frame while he stands and Skyrim shows it again for crouch and prone.
- A punch near an oil puddle no longer sets it alight under the player: destructibles lying flat on the floor are left out of punches and throws.
- Vehicles no longer die on roads far below where the player was put down: the collision island's origin moved from 100 m to 1000 m up, since MSC treats anything below about -5 m as under water and its AirIntake check flooded the van's engine there.
- The moped's suspension travel is 1.6 times MSC's instead of twice, and its front fork is softer than the rear (0.75 times the spring rate).
- The ridden moped balances on the ground: its roll is eased toward the lean the turn asks for (upright going straight), so it no longer always falls onto its side; past 70° it is left to fall. Its center of mass sits 15 cm below where MSC puts it, so bumps no longer pitch it over the front wheel. The balance is strong enough to beat gravity, its pitch is damped on the ground, and the last 40% of each wheel's travel pushes back progressively instead of hitting UnityCar's bump stop, which kicked it up at every bump.
- The van holds Skyrim's steep roads: 1.3 times the tire grip forward and sideways, and 0.75 times the rear brake, so it no longer spins its wheels uphill or slides its back out braking downhill.
- Alpha-blended MSC model surfaces (the vehicles' glass) no longer write depth, so they don't hide other see-through things behind them or the fog.

- Riding the Jonnez moped and seated cameras (protocol v30, `VehicleState.seat` with `kVehicleSeat`, `PlayerState.viewHeading`/`viewPitch` always sent): the moped keeps MSC's own mass and center of mass (MSC balances it by moving the center: -0.1 m while ridden, 0.4 m at speed), where PerkeleRim's tune put back a fixed one every second that left the ridden moped 20 cm top-heavy, and the 1.2x mass stalled its engine; it gets 0.6x spring rate, 2x travel, 0.8x compression and 1.5x rebound damping, since its 8 cm bottomed out in road holes, the bump stop threw it up and the fork sprang back over the handlebar; and it lands its jumps: once all its wheels are off the ground for 0.15 s while ridden, its spin is eased toward level and facing the way it flies (`msc/src/Game/AirAssist.cs`, log `air assist: landed after N s`), where before it rolled in the air and came down on its side; the parked moped (light enough to lift by hand) no longer counts as a loose item, which re-read its whole model for half a second every few seconds while riding; the van's and the Kekmet's lowered center now follows wherever MSC moves it. `scripts/spike.ps1 -Command 'fsmvar <object> <fsm> <variable> <value>'` sets an FSM variable (`fsmvar FPSCamera Hangover TimeLeft 0` ends MSC's hangover shaking). Skyrim's camera turns to where MSC's camera itself looks (in a rolled vehicle the player's yaw and the camera's pitch missed it by degrees, so the controls were off the crosshair), and a driven vehicle hangs around the camera by the seat MSC measures every frame instead of one Skyrim learned while stopped, so the controls stay put while leaning, on the moped and when tilted. MSC logs how a driven or just righted vehicle rides.

- Standing next to the garage's shelves could count as having fallen through the ground and put the player on the shed's beams; only a player who isn't standing on anything is put back on top now.
- Starving (or dying of thirst, stress, a full bladder, drowning, a wasp or the train) in MSC while handed over left MSC's player lying on the ground for good, even after reloading in Skyrim: MSC's death screen object was switched off a frame late, after it had already taken the player apart, and MSC's needs stopped for the rest of the session. These deaths are now kept from starting at all (Skyrim still takes the hit) and MSC's needs carry on.

- Left click punched instead of picking up items MSC shows no prompt for (empty bottles): it now stays MSC's click whenever something MSC would pick up is under the crosshair.
- Left click sometimes punched instead of picking up an item the crosshair was on: MSC's hand icon flickers on and off from frame to frame, and a click that landed in an off frame counted as free hands. A prompt seen in the last half second now keeps the click MSC's. Spike `spawn <product> [n]` puts a fresh shop product (sausages, pizza, cigarettes, ...) in front of the player, and `global` sets int globals too (`global PlayerCigarettes 20`).

- Enemies walked up to the handed-over player and never fought: Skyrim treated a player with its movement and look controls off like one in a scripted scene. The controls now stay on and the input handlers for keys MSC uses refuse instead (Z no longer shouts, Ctrl no longer sneaks, F no longer switches to third person).
- MSC's models in Skyrim were striped and oddly lit: the vertex layout put the UV, normal and tangent offsets one slot too low, so Skyrim read the UVs from the position bytes. Built with CommonLib's `SetAttributeOffset` now.
